# CareFlow Stakeholder Demo — Narration Script

Use this script when recording voiceover for the automated demo video (`npm run demo:record`). Timestamps are approximate and align with pause durations in `frontend/e2e/helpers/demo.ts`.

**Target length:** ~8–10 minutes  
**Tone:** Confident, clear, stakeholder-focused — emphasize business outcomes, not technical implementation.

---

## Scene 1 — Introduction (0:00 – 0:45)

**Screen:** Login page

> Welcome to CareFlow — a modern clinic management platform built for healthcare providers who need one place to run their daily operations.
>
> CareFlow brings together patient records, appointment scheduling, visit documentation, doctor management, and inventory — all in a secure, role-based web portal.
>
> Let's start by signing in as the owner of Sunrise Dental Clinic.

---

## Scene 2 — Dashboard (0:45 – 1:30)

**Screen:** Clinic owner dashboard after login

> This is the clinic dashboard — the command center for day-to-day operations.
>
> At a glance, leadership can see total patients, new registrations, and appointment volume. Interactive charts show appointment trends, patient demographics, and revenue over time.
>
> On the right, today's schedule gives front-desk staff immediate visibility into who is coming in — with quick access to each visit.

---

## Scene 3 — Patients (1:30 – 2:20)

**Screen:** Patients list → patient detail

> Patient management is central to CareFlow. Staff can search and browse the full patient registry, with age, gender, blood group, and last visit date at their fingertips.
>
> Opening a patient profile shows their complete history — every appointment linked back to the visit chart. This gives doctors and receptionists full context before the patient walks in.

---

## Scene 4 — Appointments & Visit Chart (2:20 – 4:10)

**Screen:** Appointments list → visit detail (scrolling through sections)

> The appointments module handles the full lifecycle — from booking to check-in, documentation, and completion.
>
> Staff can filter by date, type, and status to find any visit quickly.
>
> The visit chart is where clinical work happens. Doctors capture the reason for visit, medical history, vitals with automatic BMI calculation, procedures, prescribed medicines, uploaded documents, and itemized charges.
>
> Visits move through a clear workflow — confirmed, in progress, and completed — with the chart locking once the visit is done. Summary and prescription PDFs can be generated on the spot.

---

## Scene 5 — Calendar (4:10 – 4:50)

**Screen:** Calendar (Month view)

> The calendar gives a visual view of the clinic schedule across all doctors.
>
> Switch between month, week, and day views. Color-coded slots show appointment status at a glance. Staff can book new appointments or open existing visits directly from the calendar.

---

## Scene 6 — Doctors (4:50 – 5:20)

**Screen:** Doctors list

> CareFlow makes it easy to manage the clinical team. The doctor roster shows each provider's specialty, contact details, and active status.
>
> Clinic owners can onboard new doctors, update profiles, and activate or deactivate staff as the team changes.

---

## Scene 7 — Inventory (5:20 – 5:50)

**Screen:** Inventory list with stat cards

> Inventory tracking keeps clinics stocked and aware. Dashboard cards highlight total items, available stock, low-stock warnings, and critical shortages.
>
> Staff can add items, update quantities, and monitor availability percentages — reducing the risk of running out of essential supplies during patient care.

---

## Scene 8 — User Management (5:50 – 6:40)

**Screen:** Staff list → Roles list

> Access control is built in. Clinic owners manage staff accounts and assign roles with granular permissions.
>
> The staff module handles onboarding, profile management, and activation. The roles module lets administrators define what each team member can see and do — from front-desk reception to clinical staff.

---

## Scene 9 — Platform Administration (6:40 – 8:20)

**Screen:** Super admin login → Clinics → clinic detail → Settings

> CareFlow is also a multi-clinic platform. Platform administrators get a dedicated view to manage every clinic on the system.
>
> The clinics dashboard shows registered businesses, their type, owner, employee count, and subscription status. Drilling into a clinic reveals full profile details, subscription management, addresses, and the employee roster.
>
> Platform settings control global policies — like the default free-trial period for new clinics — giving operators centralized control over the entire network.

---

## Scene 10 — Closing (8:20 – 8:40)

**Screen:** Clinics list (closing frame)

> CareFlow brings together everything a clinic needs — patients, appointments, clinical documentation, team management, and inventory — in one secure, modern platform.
>
> Whether you run a single practice or manage a network of clinics, CareFlow scales with you.
>
> Thank you for watching.

---

## Recording tips

- Speak at a natural pace (~140–160 words per minute)
- Pause briefly at scene transitions to match on-screen navigation
- If the automated recording runs faster or slower than these timestamps, re-record voiceover in sections and align in your video editor
- Background music (optional): keep it low — stakeholders should hear the narration clearly
